Cabin Euchre Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Cabin Euchre is a card game made by Gccody. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and your information is never sold. This page explains the little that does leave your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, saved game, stats and trophies are kept in the game's own storage on your device. They aren't sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

Online play

If you play online, the game connects to our room server, which runs on Cloudflare. This only happens when you use PLAY ONLINE. The server handles:

  • The name you choose, up to 10 letters and digits. It doesn't have to be your real name. It's shown to the other people at your table, and to the host of a table you ask to join.
  • The game itself: the table's code, the cards and the moves.
  • A random seat token, which lets you return to your seat if you get disconnected. It isn't tied to you or your device, and it's kept on your device so you can rejoin.
  • Your IP address, which any internet connection reveals. It's used to limit how fast connections can be made, to protect the server from abuse. Cloudflare may also keep it for a short time in its operational logs.

A table's information is deleted about an hour after everyone has left, or after 24 hours without play.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, trophies you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center if you're signed in, and trophies you've already earned there come back to the game. Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy. We don't receive your Game Center identity.

Children

The game doesn't ask for anyone's age or personal information, and there's no chat. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can play without going online, and you can use any name you like when you do. Since we don't have accounts, we can't connect anything on the server to you. If you have a question or a request, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions go to cabineuchresupport@mail.gccody.com.
