Cabin Euchre Support

Something not working, or got a question? Write to cabineuchresupport@mail.gccody.com. Tell us what device you're on and what happened, and we'll take a look.

How do I learn to play?

Choose LEARN TO PLAY on the title screen, and Benny will walk you through a real match. RULES has the illustrated How to Play if you'd rather read.

How do I play with friends?

Choose PLAY ONLINE, then HOST A TABLE, and share the 6-letter code on the table. Your friends choose JOIN WITH A CODE and type it in. Bots fill any empty seats, and only the host can deal.

I got disconnected. Can I get my seat back?

Yes. Open PLAY ONLINE and choose REJOIN TABLE, or type the same code again. A bot plays for you while you're gone, and after about 10 minutes away your seat goes to the bot for good.

Will I lose my game if I close the app?

No. A game against the bots saves itself, and CONTINUE on the title screen picks it back up.

Why aren't my trophies showing in Game Center?

Check that you're signed in under Settings, Game Center. The game keeps every trophy on your device and sends them all each time it starts, so anything earned while you were signed out or offline goes up the next time.

I got a new phone. Where did my stats go?

Trophies come back from Game Center. Your stats, like games played and win streaks, are only kept on the device, so they start over.

Privacy

The game has no accounts, ads or tracking. The privacy policy has the details.
